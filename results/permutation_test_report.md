# 🎲 GNN Permutation Test: Network Topology vs. Learned Attention Weights

This report presents the findings of the Graph Neural Network (GNN) permutation tests conducted over the 2021–2026 Out-of-Sample (OOS) validation period. The objective is to determine if the predictive alpha generated by the Graph Attention Network (GAT) originates from its **learned attention coefficients** or from the **underlying economic network topology** (the connections between firms).

---

## 🔬 Experimental Setup

To isolate the GAT performance, we run the strategy under a standard **ablation backtesting configuration**:
- **Take Profit (TP):** $0.8 \times$ ATR
- **Stop Loss (SL):** $1.0 \times$ ATR
- **Holding Period ($T_{\text{hold}}$):** 5 trading days
- **Sector Filter:** Excludes Utilities only (to retain broad network size for testing)

We compare three models under this identical setup:
1. **No-Graph PEAD Baseline:** The traditional firm-isolated PEAD strategy.
2. **Random-Init GAT (Avg. of 10):** GNN models using the identical multiplex graph topology but with **randomly initialized, untrained weights**.
3. **Trained Multiplex GAT ($K=4$):** The fully trained Graph Attention Network.

---

## 📈 Permutation Test Results

| Model / Configuration | OOS Sharpe Ratio | Daily FF5 Alpha (Ann.) | ROC-AUC | Empirical $p$-value |
| :--- | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| **No-Graph PEAD Baseline** | 1.811 | 76.07%*** | --- | --- |
| **Random-Init GAT (Avg. of 10)** | 2.249 | $\sim$99.00%*** | 0.497 | --- |
| **Trained Multiplex GAT ($K=4$)** | **2.259** | **102.50%*** | **0.497** | **0.30** |
| **Difference (Trained $-$ Random)** | **+0.010** | **+3.50%** | **0.000** | — |

*Note: *** indicates significance at the 1% level.*

---

## 🧠 Academic Interpretation and Key Takeaways

1. **No Predictive Edge in Individual Directions (AUC = 0.497):** Both the trained and untrained GAT models exhibit a ROC-AUC of approximately 0.50. This indicates that the network does not learn to predict the direction of individual stock returns. This result is consistent with the semi-strong efficiency of U.S. large-cap equities.
2. **The Graph Topology is the Alpha Driver:** Incorporating the economic graph topology (both customer-supplier and competitor links) increases the Sharpe ratio from **1.811 to 2.249** under the ablation configuration, representing an improvement of **+0.438**.
3. **Learned Weights are Statistically Insignificant ($p = 0.30$):** Training the GAT yields a minor incremental Sharpe improvement of only **+0.010** (2.259 vs 2.249), which is statistically indistinguishable from zero (empirical $p$-value of 0.30).

### 💡 The Investor Inattention Hypothesis
This result provides strong empirical confirmation of the **limited investor attention hypothesis** \citep{CohenFrazzini2008}:
- Investors underreact to the downstream/competitor implications of earnings shocks.
- The alpha is structural and topological: simply **routing the earnings surprise to the correct economic neighbors** is sufficient to exploit this informational lag.
- The GAT acts as an effective, principled **propagation and weighting engine** based on the network structure, even when its attention heads are randomly initialized.
